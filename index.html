About

Angel Wen is a Product Designer with 10+ years of experience designing complex software applications — from enterprise platforms at Hootsuite to consumer products and AI-powered tools.

Currently focused on the intersection of AI and spatial computing, designing hands-free, multimodal interactions that move beyond traditional screens. Recent work includes building 20 XR applications in 30 days, exploring gesture-based interfaces, body tracking, and AI/ML-powered experiences.

As a violinist of 20+ years, design is informed by a deep understanding of how physical movement translates to digital interaction — the foundation of the Invisible Violin and other spatial instruments.

Philosophy

The best AI interfaces don't feel like AI interfaces. They feel like natural extensions of human capability — intuitive, contextual, and invisible until needed. This is especially true for communal and shared environments like the home, where technology should serve multiple users with varying needs and contexts.
